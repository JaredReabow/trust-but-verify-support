System unattended monitor

Trust But Verify

A local, consent-first monitor for a Mac or PC you deliberately leave unattended.

Watch only when you arm it

Arm the skull in the menu bar or system tray, or let the optional inactivity timer do it. A click or key press while armed can flash every screen red, play a deliberately harsh alarm, show a warning, and save screenshots plus camera photos locally.

Choose silent evidence mode, select individual screens and cameras, set your own activation and deactivation gestures, and decide whether a disarm still saves evidence.
