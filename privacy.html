Trust But Verify

Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

What the app does

Trust But Verify starts disarmed. The owner must arm it from the menu bar or tray, or opt into a user-defined inactivity timer. While armed, it observes the timing and type of pointer and keyboard activity and matches the activation and deactivation gestures the owner configured.

The app does not store typed characters or build a key log.

Screenshots and camera photos

When the configured event occurs, the app may save screenshots of the selected displays and photographs from the selected cameras. These files are stored locally in the current user's application-data directory. The owner chooses whether to use all detected devices or only the primary display and default camera.

Data collection and sharing

The app contains no network client, developer account, analytics, advertising, or upload service. The developer does not receive the screenshots, camera images, settings, or activity data.

Control and deletion

The owner can change every behavior in Settings, disable camera or screenshot capture, use silent evidence mode, open the evidence folder, or delete all local evidence. Store and operating-system permission controls can revoke camera and screen access at any time.

Children and consent

Trust But Verify is an administrative utility, not a child-directed service. Use it only on a computer you own or are authorized to administer, and follow applicable consent and recording laws.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be opened through the support page.
